# Welcome to AMITY!

A world of voxels to explore and conquer.

## Building

- Blocks are half a metre across, so you can build finely detailed things.
- Right click places **one** block. Hold **SPRINT** as well to keep placing, for walls and bridges.
- Breaking never stops while you hold the button.
- You can place blocks into water to build out into lakes.

## Inventory
Left click to pick up and put down a whole stack
Right click to pick up half a stack and put down one item

## Creative mode

- **Double-tap Jump** to start or stop flying.

## The world

- The sun and moon follow real paths across the sky, and the seasons turn: summer days are long and bright, winter days short and low.
- If you stop moving for a moment when you arrive somewhere new, the world is still loading around you.
